Our Story

Small
City,
Big Vision

MK Freelance Studio ki shuruat 2020 mein Nanauta, Saharanpur se hui — ek laptop, ek internet connection, aur ek sapna: local businesses ko global digital presence dena.

Humne dekha tha ki hamare aas-paas ke businessmen — chahe kirana store ho ya manufacturing unit — sab online hona chahte the, lekin koi affordable aur trustworthy option nahi tha. Waheen se MK Studio ka idea aaya.

"Mera goal sirf websites banana nahi — businesses ko grow karna hai."
— Mohammad Kaif, Founder

Aaj hum website design se lekar 3D web experiences, SEO, Meta Ads, Google Ads aur social media management tak — sab kuch under one roof provide karte hain.

Our Mission
India ke har chhote-bade business ko premium digital services provide karna — affordable rates par, with guaranteed results. No jargon, no hidden costs.
Our Vision
Uttar Pradesh ke har district mein ek strong digital presence banana — aur India ko world-class digital talent se connect karna, starting from Nanauta.
Our Values
Transparency, punctuality, aur real results. Hum woh nahi kehte jo aap sunna chahte hain — hum woh karte hain jo aapke business ke liye sahi hai.
